# VEYRA Product Vision

## Positioning

**VEYRA — Intelligent Living. Unified Operations.**

VEYRA is a configurable property and community operations platform that helps property management organisations operate residential communities securely, transparently, and efficiently.

It serves condominiums first, while the underlying product model is designed to expand to apartment communities, gated communities, residential estates, and multi-property operators.

## The problem

Residential property operations are often fragmented across spreadsheets, paper forms, messaging groups, phone calls, standalone accounting tools, visitor books, and disconnected vendor processes. This creates:

- Incomplete or inconsistent resident and unit records
- Slow visitor verification and weak traceability
- Poor visibility into maintenance requests and service quality
- Manual billing and reconciliation
- Unclear approval ownership
- Fragmented resident communication
- Weak asset and contract lifecycle tracking
- Limited management reporting
- Difficult onboarding and offboarding of residents and staff

VEYRA should create a reliable operational record and workflow for each important activity.

## Product promise

A property operator should be able to understand the status of its community, act on operational work, communicate with residents, and produce trustworthy records from one platform.

Residents should be able to complete common tasks without visiting an office or chasing staff.

Security and operations staff should receive simple, role-appropriate tools that work under real-world conditions.

## Target customers

1. Single condominium management corporations
2. Professional property management companies
3. Multi-building residential developments
4. Gated communities and residential estates
5. Multi-property operators

## Personas

- Organisation owner / platform operator
- Property manager
- Management committee member
- Finance officer
- Security supervisor and guard
- Maintenance supervisor and technician
- Resident owner
- Tenant
- Authorised household member
- Vendor / contractor

A person may hold multiple roles and relationships. Roles and access scopes must be modelled independently from a person's identity.

## Product principles

- Secure by default; deny access unless explicitly granted.
- One person can have multiple relationships across properties.
- Property configuration should be flexible without allowing unsafe custom code.
- Financial records must be traceable and correctable through controlled reversals, not destructive edits.
- Every important workflow has a clear owner, state, timestamp, and history.
- Mobile and low-friction workflows matter for residents and frontline staff.
- Integrations must be replaceable through adapters.
- AI may assist but cannot bypass policy, permissions, or approvals.

## Success measures

Measure outcomes, not just feature counts:

- Resident onboarding completion rate
- Percentage of visitor arrivals processed digitally
- Median time to verify a visitor
- Maintenance first-response and resolution times
- SLA attainment
- Invoice collection rate and days outstanding
- Payment reconciliation exception rate
- Facility booking utilisation
- Announcement delivery and acknowledgement rates
- Staff task completion rate
- Support requests per property
- Platform availability and critical incident rate
- Tenant-isolation and access-control test coverage

Baseline these metrics during pilots before setting targets.

## Out of scope for the first release

Unless the pilot customer explicitly requires them:
- Full ERP/general ledger replacement
- Payroll processing
- Building automation control
- Facial recognition or biometric identity systems
- Automated enforcement decisions
- AI making payments, granting access, or approving expenses
- Complex marketplace or social networking features
